Step 0. This is a continuation
You now rebuild the layout of the same site with Bootstrap. Same team, same theme, same repository - the theme is locked until the end of the course and you do not create new pages. Your HTML stays as it is: you may add classes, and wrap existing content in the container, row and column elements the grid needs, but you do not replace your structure and you do not start a page over. The site still runs from local files; no domain, no hosting.
The rule for this assignment: Bootstrap builds, your CSS corrects
Bootstrap must do the heavy work - the page structure, the columns, the spacing, the navigation, the buttons and the responsive behaviour all come from its classes. Your own stylesheet becomes a small correction layer on top of it: your brand colours, your fonts, and the few details Bootstrap does not cover. As a guide, expect to keep well under a hundred lines of your own CSS in the whole project. If a block can be built with Bootstrap classes and you built it by hand instead, it is wrong even when it looks right.
Connecting Bootstrap
Link Bootstrap from a CDN in the head of every page, and link your own stylesheet after it, so that your rules win where they conflict. You may also link the Bootstrap JavaScript bundle - you are not writing any JavaScript, but some components need it. Write in a comment on each page which version of Bootstrap you are using.
Containers and the grid
Every page sits inside a container. Use both container and container-fluid somewhere in the project and put a comment where you chose one over the other, saying why. Build your content with rows and columns, and make at least three different blocks respond to screen width - for example col-12 col-md-6 col-lg-4 on a set of cards, services or prices. Use the gutter and alignment classes rather than your own margins, and show that you understand nesting by putting one row inside a column at least once.
Responsive behaviour
Your pages must work at three widths: a phone around 375px, a tablet around 768px, and a normal desktop window. Nothing may overflow sideways and no horizontal scrollbar may appear at 375px - that is the single most common failure and it costs 10 points. Your navigation collapses into a working toggler on small screens. Use the responsive display and text-alignment utilities to hide, show or re-align at least two elements at different breakpoints.
Typography, buttons and utilities
Style your text with Bootstrap classes - display headings, lead, text alignment and colour, muted and small text - instead of writing your own font rules. Use at least four button classes, including one outline style, one size variant and one disabled state, and make sure every button is a real link or a real form control, not decoration. Across the project use at least ten different utility classes covering spacing, colour or background, borders, shadow, and flex or display - and use them on real elements, not on empty ones added to tick a box.
One Bootstrap component
Add one component from the Bootstrap documentation that genuinely suits your project - cards, accordion, carousel, alert, badge, breadcrumb, table styling, list group or modal. Copy its markup from the documentation, then adapt it to your own content, and write a comment above it naming the component and what you changed.
Cleaning up your old CSS
Bootstrap now does much of what you wrote in Assignment 2. Delete the rules it replaced - your hand-written grid, most of your spacing and alignment, your button styling - and keep a short file listing what you removed and which Bootstrap class took its place. A stylesheet that still fights Bootstrap with its own layout rules is the main thing I will be looking for. Your remaining CSS may set colours, fonts and small corrections, and may override a Bootstrap rule where you can justify it in a comment.
Forbidden
No downloaded themes, templates or admin dashboards, no other CSS framework, no site builders, no JavaScript of your own. Do not rebuild the grid by hand with float or with your own flex rules - that costs 10 points each time. Inline style attributes and !important cost 5 points each; if you need one, Bootstrap already has a utility class for it.
Screenshots instead of a report
There is no written report in this assignment. Submit three screenshots of one page at the three widths above, plus one screenshot of your navigation collapsed on a phone width. Nothing else is written up - everything else is checked in your code and at the defense.
Submission
The same Git repository, extended: your updated pages, your reduced stylesheet, the list of what you removed, the four screenshots, an updated AI log and README. Everyone commits from their own account - at least four commits across three different days, six if you work alone. Your HTML must still pass the W3C validator with zero errors.
